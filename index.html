Off The Court

Bio

Joseph Haubert is a 2026 graduate of Regis Jesuit High School in Aurora, Colorado, and will continue his academic and basketball career at Coe College in Cedar Rapids, Iowa.

A three-year varsity player, Joseph is known for his leadership, court vision, ball-handling, basketball IQ, and ability to create opportunities for his teammates. He approaches the game with discipline, composure, and a team-first mindset.

Beyond basketball, Joseph has been actively involved in leadership and service through the VIA Leadership Program, the Grounds Crew Leadership Team, St. Thomas More Retreats, Fellowship of Christian Athletes, and Future Business Leaders of America. His commitment to serving others earned him the 2025 Regis Jesuit Loyola Leadership Award and the 2026 Raider Man of the Year Award.

Joseph was also recognized as a two-time CHSAA Academic All-State First Team selection and as a Silver Star Award recipient while balancing the demands of academics, athletics, part-time employment, and extracurricular involvement.

His interests include medicine, law, agriculture, public policy, and theology. Guided by his Catholic faith, Joseph is committed to growing as a student, athlete, and leader while making a positive impact on his teammates, campus, and community.
